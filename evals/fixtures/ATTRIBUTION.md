## Licence notes

- **CC0** — public-domain dedication, no attribution required (credited anyway).
- **CC BY-SA 4.0** — `l-shaped-plan.jpg` requires attribution and share-alike on any
  redistribution of the image itself. Attribution is recorded in the table above.

If you would rather not carry a share-alike image in the repository, delete
`l-shaped-plan.jpg` and its manifest entry; the runner skips fixtures whose images are
absent, and every other fixture is CC0 or public domain.
